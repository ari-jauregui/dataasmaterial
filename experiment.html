Welcome to 2055! A future with equity.

You’re about to see 300+ speculative newspaper headlines projecting a more equitable future from 2055. This experience is intended as a planning and advocacy tool to imagine alternative futures where the end of the world doesn’t seem more likely than the end of capitalism.

This future is measured by the Index of Global Equity (0-100), an average of multiple sub-indices for gender, LGBTIQ+, land, education, health, housing, environment, justice equity. The higher the score, the more equitable the world.
